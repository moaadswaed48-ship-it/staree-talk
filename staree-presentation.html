The Cracks
Where the trial can crack
Healthy volunteers

Independent people from general practice. Not the frail 85-year-old in bed twelve.

One drug, one dose

Atorvastatin 40 mg, titrated from 20 mg. Other statins and doses are extrapolation.

What drove the benefit

One population

And it tested starting a statin, not stopping one.
The Puzzle
Where STAREE fits

Until now, statins after 70 rested on extrapolation and subgroups.

STAREE answers directly

First large primary-prevention trial dedicated to people over 70. Fewer events.

Still missing

Function and cognition: no gain shown.

My reading

Guidelines will likely strengthen event prevention after 70, with shared decision-making. Not a guideline yet.
